**0:15–0:30 — Healthmap and routing.** Show the 20 campaign rows. Point at the purple market-movement band and explain that portfolio-wide motion routes to digest. Use the donut to show the computed routing mix.

**0:30–0:47 — Alert Inbox.** Open Alert Inbox and point to the typed `TRACKING_BROKEN` row. Say: “This is not an anomaly score. It says conversions collapsed while traffic held, gives the band, z, persistence, money at risk, and the first thing to check.” Toggle the route or channel filters.

**0:47–1:00 — Shared Causes.** Open Shared Causes. Show the computed landing-page grouping. Say: “The novelty is that shared context becomes one investigation. Alert fatigue is fought by merging symptoms.”

**1:00–1:16 — Fatigue Battle.** Open Fatigue Battle and press Play. Point to the naive-versus-QuietAlert trust proxies derived from routed alert load. Say: “We expose a reproducible fatigue proxy without inventing incident recall when no labels were supplied.”

**1:16–1:30 — Honest evaluation.** Open Evaluation or Sensitivity Lab. Say: “No labels file was supplied, so we expose stability and operator-review proxies rather than fake recall. With labels, this screen becomes incident-level precision, recall, delay, and miss diagnosis. Every threshold trade-off stays visible.”
